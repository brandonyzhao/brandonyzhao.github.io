About

I am a fifth-year PhD candidate at Caltech in the Computing and Mathematical Sciences department. I mainly work on computational imaging, with a focus on astrophysical applications. More broadly, I am interested in generative modeling and problems at the intersection of machine learning and physics.

I am advised by Katie Bouman in the Computational Cameras Group, supported by the Gupta Fellowship and a NASA PDRDF grant. I am currently also a student researcher at Google Deepmind, hosted by Pratul Srinivasan.

I am a member of the Event Horizon Telescope collaboration. I am also currently in collaboration with the James Webb Space Telescope working on dark matter imaging.
